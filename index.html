01 / MECHANICAL DESIGN CONCEPT DESIGN

Listening beneath
the surface.

A conceptual autonomous marine buoy for monitoring fish and lobster activity. Acoustic sensing, onboard storage, and wireless communication in a sealed, field-deployable platform.

SolidWorks Mechanical design Ocean technology
Explore the design +

A modular electronics chassis and 96-cell battery pack sit inside an O-ring-sealed pressure housing. The design includes a 15 psi pressure relief valve and a hydrophone specified for 10 Hz–200 kHz sensing at depths up to 100 m.

Eight 64 GB SD cards provide onboard storage. A surface antenna, navigation light, handling rings, and hydrophone guard support deployment and retrieval.
