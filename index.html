01 / SOFTWARE + DRONESPHOTOGRAMMETRY

Drone Video to 3D Simulation Software

A photogrammetry platform that turns drone video into interactive 3D reconstructions.

Explore the software +

TECHNOLOGIES & METHODS

PythonTypeScriptReactThree.jsFastAPISQLiteOpenCVFFmpegCOLMAPCUDA

I developed a full-stack platform that takes drone video from upload through reconstruction, analysis, and export. An automated computer-vision pipeline extracts and filters frames, estimates camera positions, and generates sparse or dense point clouds and meshes, with CUDA acceleration for dense reconstruction. The interactive 3D viewer supports real-world scaling, measurements, elevation analysis, annotations, and point-cloud cleanup. A Smart Retry system diagnoses incomplete reconstructions and reruns only the processing stages needed to improve the result. The backend tracks projects, jobs, and reconstruction history so users can compare runs and export their results.

02 / MECHANICAL DESIGNCONCEPT DESIGN

Marine Monitoring Buoy

A conceptual acoustic monitoring buoy for fish and lobster habitats.

Explore the design +

TECHNOLOGIES & METHODS

SolidWorksMechanical designAcoustic sensingSubsystem integration

I designed a conceptual autonomous marine buoy in SolidWorks to support underwater acoustic monitoring. A sealed pressure housing with O-ring endcaps protects a modular battery pack, electronics chassis, and onboard data storage. I integrated the hydrophone, pressure relief valve, electrical connections, and anode into the endcaps while considering sealing and maintenance access. The surface structure supports the underwater housing and incorporates a navigation light, WiFi hardware, and hydrophone protection. Building the complete assembly let me evaluate component fit, clearances, and the overall mechanical layout.

04 / ROBOTICS + CONTROLSAUTONOMOUS DELIVERY

NavBot — Autonomous Medication Delivery System

An autonomous robot designed to deliver medication along trained routes.

Explore the robot +

TECHNOLOGIES & METHODS

C++VEX IQSolidWorksDijkstra’s algorithmPI/P controlSensor integrationEmbedded robotics

I developed NavBot to transport medication along trained routes and reduce repetitive hospital delivery tasks. I built and refined a VEX-based chassis and added a motorized medication box with an automatic lid and locking mechanism. Training and Autonomous modes in C++ record routes for replay, while Dijkstra’s algorithm selects the shortest available path between stored locations. Proportional and PI heading control correct motor output to reduce drift during straight-line driving and turns. Distance, bumper, touch, inertial, and motor feedback support obstacle detection, collision response, and medication-box operation.
