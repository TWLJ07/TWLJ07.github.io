01 / SOFTWARE + DRONESPHOTOGRAMMETRY
+ FROM DRONE VIDEO TO 3D RECONSTRUCTION

Drone Video to 3D Simulation Software

A full-stack photogrammetry platform that transforms raw drone video into interactive 3D reconstructions, covering video upload, processing, visualization, analysis, and export.

PythonTypeScriptReactThree.jsFastAPISQLiteOpenCVFFmpegCOLMAPCUDA
Explore the software +
  • Automated vision pipeline. FFmpeg, OpenCV, and COLMAP handle frame extraction, image-quality filtering, feature detection and matching, camera pose estimation, sparse reconstruction, and CUDA-accelerated dense reconstruction.
  • Reconstruction outputs. Sparse and dense point clouds, CPU-generated sparse meshes, and Poisson dense meshes have dedicated processing timelines and quality controls for each stage.
  • Interactive analysis. A React and Three.js environment supports camera trajectories, real-world scaling, distance and area measurements, elevation analysis, point inspection, annotations, and point-cloud cleanup.
  • Smart Retry. The system analyzes image registration, feature coverage, verified-match connectivity, reconstruction quality, and previous retry results to diagnose failed or partial reconstructions and selectively rerun only the necessary stages.
  • Project and job management. A FastAPI and SQLite backend manages projects, reconstruction jobs, processing state, artifacts, and reconstruction history.
  • Comparison and export. Tools compare reconstruction runs and export point clouds, meshes, measurements, maps, and reconstruction reports.

02 / MECHANICAL DESIGNCONCEPT DESIGN

Marine Monitoring Buoy

A conceptual autonomous marine monitoring buoy designed in SolidWorks for localized fish and lobster habitat monitoring through underwater acoustic sensing.

SolidWorksMechanical designAcoustic sensingSubsystem integration
Explore the design +
  • Sealed pressure housing. O-ring endcaps protect the battery system, electronics chassis, and data-storage hardware inside the underwater housing.
  • Modular internal layout. A high-capacity battery pack, WiFi PCB, power and control electronics, and multiple SD cards provide power, communication, and onboard acoustic data storage.
  • Integrated endcaps. The hydrophone, pressure relief valve, electrical connections, and anode are positioned with sealing, component placement, and accessibility in mind.
  • Surface structure. The buoy supports the underwater housing while incorporating a navigation light, WiFi communication hardware, and hydrophone protection.
  • Complete assembly. Individual SolidWorks part models and subsystem integration enable evaluation of packaging, fit, component clearances, and the overall mechanical architecture.
03 / DESIGN + FABRICATIONBUILT & TESTED

SD Card & Battery Pack Test Fixtures

Two custom test fixtures designed, manufactured, and validated to standardize electrical testing with repeatable, easy-to-use setups for SD cards and battery packs.

SolidWorksElectrical integrationManufacturingFunctional testing
Explore the fixtures +
  • Component integration. Existing PCBs, switches, power and Ethernet connectors, SD card hardware, banana jacks, LEDs, and voltage displays are integrated into custom fixture designs.
  • Mechanical design. Custom mounting plates, supports, enclosures, and complete SolidWorks assemblies are supported by detailed part and assembly drawings for manufacturing.
  • Electrical documentation. Wiring diagrams for both fixtures define component connections and support assembly, testing, and future troubleshooting.
  • Hands-on fabrication. Manufacturing and assembly involved drilling, soldering, wiring, component mounting, and mechanical assembly, alongside sourcing and ordering components.
  • Validation. Both completed fixtures underwent electrical continuity and connection checks, functional testing, and verification of SD card and battery pack testing operations.

04 / ROBOTICS + CONTROLSAUTONOMOUS DELIVERY

NavBot — Autonomous Medication Delivery System

An autonomous medical delivery robot designed to reduce repetitive hospital tasks by transporting medication along trained routes, with autonomous navigation and obstacle-safety features.

C++VEX IQSolidWorksDijkstra’s algorithmPI/P controlSensor integrationEmbedded robotics
Explore the robot +
  • Chassis and drivetrain. A rigid VEX-based chassis was designed and constructed, with iterations to the frame, wheel supports, and component mounts to improve stability, weight distribution, and driving performance.
  • Medication access. A motor-actuated box uses a hinged lid, lever-style opening mechanism, and secondary motorized lock to securely store medication and automatically provide access at delivery locations.
  • Train and replay. Training and Autonomous modes programmed in C++ let operators manually train routes while recording drive distances and headings, then autonomously replay stored routes.
  • Shortest-path planning. Dijkstra’s algorithm uses an adjacency-matrix representation of stored routes to determine and execute the shortest available path between hospital locations.
  • Closed-loop control. Proportional and PI heading control dynamically correct motor output during straight-line driving and turns to reduce drift and improve autonomous path accuracy.
  • Sensor feedback. Distance, bumper, touch, inertial, and motor feedback support obstacle detection, collision response, heading control, and automated medication-box operation.
