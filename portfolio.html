Selected projects

Autonomous Marine Monitoring Buoy

Conceptual mechanical design · SolidWorks · Ocean technology

A conceptual autonomous buoy for localized monitoring of fish and lobster activity. The design integrates acoustic sensing, onboard data storage, wireless communication, and a sealed pressure-resistant housing.

  • O-ring-sealed cylindrical pressure housing with precision-machined endcaps and handling rings.
  • Pressure relief valve specified at 15 psi cracking pressure and 5 L/min vent rate.
  • Hydrophone specified for 10 Hz–200 kHz sensing at depths up to 100 m.
  • 96-cell battery pack and modular electronics chassis with eight 64 GB SD cards.
  • WiFi and power PCBs, surface antenna, navigation light, and protective hydrophone guard.

The design emphasizes modularity, maintainability, and protection in harsh marine environments. See the website for assembly and component CAD images.
